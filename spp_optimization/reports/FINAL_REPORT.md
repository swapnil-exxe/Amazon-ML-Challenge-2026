## 3. Section-by-Section Engineering Report

### A. Baseline Reproduction
- **Population Size**: 2,206,821 Training Ground Truth S1 entities / 1,732,544 Test S1 query entities.
- **Measured Ground Truth Pairs**: 7,638,365 pairs.
- **Reachable Ground Truth Pairs**: 5,966,972 pairs.
- **Missing Ground Truth Pairs**: 1,671,393 pairs.
- **Freshly Reproduced Candidate Blocking Recall**: **78.1184%** (US: 76.9604%, India: 79.8513%).
- **Candidates / Query**: Average `281.73`, Median `350.0`, P95 `350.0`, Max `350`.
- **Zero-Candidate Queries**: `152,282` (6.90%).

### B. Historical Metric Reconciliation
- **Blocking Recall**: Previously reported `78.12%`, freshly reproduced `78.1184%` (Exact match).
- **Macro F0.5**: Baseline `0.7601`, V2 Candidate `0.7854`.
- **S++ Experimental Score ($0.8339$)**: Audited and confirmed as an artifact of a 2.27% sample training subset and threshold tuning on evaluation split. Invalidated for production comparison.

### C. Root-Cause Blocking Failure Analysis
Measured on all 1,671,393 missed ground truth validation pairs:
1. **Candidate Cap Truncation (Cap $\ge 350$)**: **939,539 pairs (56.21% of missed, 12.30% of total GT)**. True matches were generated by blocking rules but fell outside the top-350 cutoff.
2. **Zero Candidate Generation**: **527,343 pairs (31.55% of missed, 6.90% of total GT)**. Zero candidates were generated because no name or address token matched the inverted index.
3. **Sparse Token / Index Truncation**: **204,511 pairs (12.24% of missed, 2.68% of total GT)**. Queries generated <350 candidates, but true match was excluded due to term frequency posting list limits.

### D. Zero-Candidate Analysis
- **Zero-Candidate Queries**: 152,282 queries (6.90%).
- **Ground Truth Loss**: 527,343 missed pairs.
- **Primary Causes**: Transliterated names (e.g. non-Latin to Latin spellings), heavy typos, and missing address fields.

### E. Candidate-Cap Analysis
- **Cap=350**: Blocking Recall = 78.12%, Avg Cands = 281.73, P95 = 350.0.
- **Cap=500**: Blocking Recall = 79.76% (+1.64% gain), Avg Cands = 421.4.
- **Cap=750**: Blocking Recall = 82.13% (+4.01% gain), Avg Cands = 612.8.
- **Cap=1000**: Blocking Recall = 84.40% (+6.28% gain), Avg Cands = 814.2.

### F & G. Normalization & Transliteration Findings
- Soft prefix indexing (4-char prefixes) and consonant-skeleton transformation (`create_consonant_skeleton`) recover zero-candidate queries, but without increasing candidate cap limits, new candidate pairs compete with exact token matches for the 350 cap space.
